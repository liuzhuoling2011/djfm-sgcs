Key -> screen direction (isometric, 瓦当镇)
W = N (north, up-screen)
A = W (west, left-screen)
S = S (south, down-screen)
D = E (east, right-screen)
WA = NW
WD = NE
SA = SW
SD = SE
Walk: hold_{W,A,S,D,WA,WD,SA,SD}.gif
Run:  run_hold_{W,A,S,D,WA,WD,SA,SD}.gif  (Shift held together with the direction keys)
